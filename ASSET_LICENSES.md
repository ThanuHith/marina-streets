# Asset licenses and attribution

## Current checkout

No external model archive could be downloaded in this environment: a direct HTTPS request to Kenney failed with an authentication error. The game therefore uses original procedural fallback models. The packs below are verified CC0 sources reserved for optional replacement models; none of their model files are currently included or used. CC0 does not require attribution, but the game credits screen lists the sources for clarity.

| Pack | Author | Official source | License | Intended use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Car Kit | Kenney | [kenney.nl/assets/car-kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit) | CC0 | Sedan and other light vehicle models |
| Animated Characters Protagonists | Kenney | [kenney.nl/assets/animated-characters-protagonists](https://kenney.nl/assets/animated-characters-protagonists) | CC0 | Pedestrian models and available walk/idle clips |
| City Kit (Suburban) | Kenney | [kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban) | CC0 | Low-rise building variants and trees |
| City Kit (Roads) | Kenney | [kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads) | CC0 | Roadside props, barriers, and road pieces |

The official pack pages identify each pack as Creative Commons CC0. No CC-BY or MIT asset is included, so no required author credit is omitted. If a different pack is substituted, verify its individual license and add its creator, source URL, license, and in-game credit requirements here before shipping it.
